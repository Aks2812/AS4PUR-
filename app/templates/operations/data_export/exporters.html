Handle the files as sensitive data. They contain user identities (names, email addresses, group memberships) and internal network destinations. The files themselves are never saved on this server: a finished export stays available for a few minutes in memory only, for your session only. Each run is recorded in the audit log (who, when, tenant, counts), never the file contents.

Private apps

One CSV with every private app, checked against the total the tenant reports. Includes each app's protocols and ports (TCP and UDP) in the format Private app definition accepts.

Users & groups

Three CSVs — users (one row per account), groups, and memberships — plus one ZIP with all three.

Off by default: only live accounts are exported. A user can have a deleted account next to a live one, so turning this on adds those rows.

Users per NPA policy

One CSV of who each NPA policy you choose applies to: its direct users, the members of its groups (each group looked up by name and expanded), and its organizational units. You pick the policies first and see what will be done before it starts; it then runs in the background with a progress bar.

Needs a token that can read NPA policies, SCIM groups and users (the users are read only when a chosen policy uses a group).

Large tenants can take up to a minute: users and groups are read a page at a time, deliberately paced to stay inside the tenant's rate limits.
