What this exports

  • Private apps — one row per private app: Netskope's own export columns, plus the protocols and ports its own export leaves out, the publishers and whether each can reach the app, policies, tags and more.
  • Users & groups — every user account and group on the tenant (SCIM, Local and AD), and which account belongs to which group.
  • Users per NPA policy — for the NPA policies you choose: the users each one lists, the members of its groups, and its organizational units.

Read-only: nothing is created, changed or deleted on the tenant. Each export is checked against the totals Netskope itself reports; if they don't match you get an explanation instead of a file, so an incomplete export is never handed over as a complete one.
